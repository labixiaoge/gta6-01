SUNHAVEN BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

You are Marco Reyes, fresh out of prison and broke in the sun-soaked port city of Sunhaven Bay. Your old friend Lena Cruz runs a garage on Harbor Row — and she has work. Steal cars, outrun the Sunhaven PD, run jobs for the Harbor Crew, and earn your way from the streets to the top of the bay.

WASD / Arrows — move & drive  ·  SHIFT — sprint
E — steal / enter / exit car  ·  SPACE — brake / handbrake
Mouse click — shoot  ·  R — reload  ·  H — honk  ·  N — mute
M — mission list  ·  P / ESC — pause
Stealing cars and shooting raises your wanted level. Lose the cops or hide at the garage.

A fully original game. Sunhaven Bay, its people and its story are invented — no Rockstar content.
